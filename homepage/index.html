~~About Me~~

As a digital artist navigating an ever changing world, I find it important to create work that reflects human experiences and resonates with others. I became an artist because I often felt trapped in my own mind while growing up. I was often overwhelmed, waves of emotions crashed over me, making it difficult to think clearly. Getting to create my artwork twisting and mashing things together allowed me to clear away the clashing voices. I have always had a strong connection to technology and I hope that my artwork inspires viewers to engage with technology in new and unique ways, challenging what humans can achieve. Ultimately, my goal as an artist is to inspire and provoke thought. I want viewers to leave my artwork with a renewed sense of curiosity about what technology can do. My portfolio reflects my journey in learning the basics and exploring technology. I hope some of the pieces I present resonate with audiences and encourage them to think about their own experiences.
