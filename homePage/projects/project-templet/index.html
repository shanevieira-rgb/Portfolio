Piece of Cake

“Piece of Cake” was a project designed to create realistic food. The title, while it refers to an easy task, is also used ironically because creating realistic food was challenging for me

Red Meat

“Red Meat” was an artwork I created as a stance on unhealthy eating habits and the corruption of the fast food industry. The title is meant to refer to the idea that red meat is bad for you

Dying Light

With this artwork, I aimed to explore light and how someone's inner light may struggle to break through the darkness.

Mercedes

With this artwork, I created a portrait of my cat, Mercedes. I wanted to change my drawing style with this piece, aiming for a look that resembles watercolors on a page.
