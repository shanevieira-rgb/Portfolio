🎬 Video Projects

Movement Project

The goal of my movement video project was to incorporate movement into the storytelling. In my video “Make a Splash,” I aimed to visualize life and the working world, filming it entirely in my backyard pool. Many people have told me that to succeed in life, you need to make a splash and establish a name for yourself. However, countless individuals spend their entire lives toiling away in jobs, hoping to make that splash, all the while unaware that they are slowly drowning in monotony.

Abstract Video Project

The goal of my abstract video project was to tell a story through shapes and sounds. I aimed to make a commentary on television and how we often jump from channel to channel, catching brief glimpses of different shows that can seem nonsensical. The video is 40 seconds long, but it feels longer because I wanted to evoke the sensation of getting lost in media, turning off your brain, and continuing to watch, unaware of the time passing.
